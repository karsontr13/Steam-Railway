# The Long Dark - Steam-Railway

> The Long Dark is a mod that adds a steam train to Great Bear Island.

*(Note: This project is in active development.*


#⚠️**Warning:** This mod changes the terrain on the island: Closed passages will now be open; destroyed bridges will be repaired; and most of the train cars that were stranded on the tracks will no longer be there. Back up your save files!

---

### ✨ Key Features

*   **Train Spawn** The train spawns in front of the ruined tunnel on the Coastal Road.
*   **Working Logic:** The train runs on coal and water. When you light the furnace, add coal, and if there is water in your boiler, steam will start to accumulate once the boiler is thoroughly heated.
*   *The train's tank holds 2000 liters of water. There are three ways to add water: First, you can fill a cauldron with up to 300 kg of snow through a hatch at the back of the train. As the cauldron heats up, the snow will slowly melt and turn into            water. Second, you can add water directly from your inventory to the tank. Third, if the train is closer than 40 meters to holes in the ice used for fishing, you can open the valve inside the cabin and draw water into the tank.
*   *If you light the stove without water in the pot, the "plug" will melt and the stove will go out. If the plug burns, you can repair it using the tool box inside the cabinet. The same applies to the pot itself.

*   **Using the Train:** When you light the stove with water in the boiler, wait a while. Once steam starts to build up, release the steam brake on the right and the handbrake at the rear. Then, adjust the gear lever on the left forward/backward and           adjust your speed by increasing or decreasing the lever in the middle. The faster you go, the faster the steam will run out. The small valve on the left draws water from the tank into the boiler, while the valve on the right increases the heat,            allowing steam to build up faster, but this will wear out the safety valve and the boiler more quickly.
*   
### ⚙️ Installation Guide

1.  Ensure you have **MelonLoader** installed on your PC.
2.  Download the latest version of the mod (`SteamRailway.dll`) from the **Releases** section.
3.  Copy the `.dll` file into the `Mods` folder in your The Long Dark directory.
